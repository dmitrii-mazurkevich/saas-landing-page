Billing

How paying for it works

Any time, in both directions. Upgrades are prorated to the day; downgrades take effect at the end of the period you've already paid for. Nobody has to email support to do it.

You're billed for the seats in use at the end of each month. Add someone for a busy fortnight and you pay for a fortnight, not a year. Seasonal teams don't get punished for it.

On annual plans, yes — bank transfer with 30-day terms, VAT handled properly for EU companies. The price is the same as paying by card; there's no surcharge for wanting a piece of paper.

50% off every plan for registered non-profits and educational institutions. Send proof of status from your organisation's domain and it's applied within a day.
