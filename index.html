What's inside

Four things that turn a shared mailbox into a system

One queue instead of four browser tabs

Email, live chat, WhatsApp, Instagram and your contact form arrive in the same place. Every conversation has one owner, so nothing is answered twice and nothing is quietly left for somebody else.

  • Collision detection — you see who else is typing
  • Private notes and @mentions on any conversation
  • Customer history and order data in the side panel
  • Works on the phone, properly — not a shrunk desktop app

Rules instead of reminders in your head

Route by keyword, language or customer plan. Escalate anything untouched for four hours. Close the "thanks!" replies automatically. You write the rule once in plain language and stop thinking about that category of work.

  • SLA timers with a visible countdown on every ticket
  • Round-robin assignment that respects working hours
  • Auto-replies that say when a real answer is coming
  • Webhooks and a REST API for anything unusual

Write the answer once

Saved replies with variables, an internal wiki and a public help centre that share the same source. Update the refund policy in one place and it changes everywhere, including the article your customers read at 2am.

  • Saved replies suggested as you type
  • Public help centre on your own domain
  • Multilingual articles with a language switcher
  • A report of what people searched for and didn't find

Numbers you can defend in a meeting

First response time, resolution time, backlog age, satisfaction, and volume by channel and by topic. Filter by team or by person, export to CSV, or have the weekly summary land in Slack on Monday morning.

  • Live dashboard that doesn't need refreshing
  • CSAT collected in the reply, not in a separate email
  • Topic clustering that shows what to fix in the product
  • Scheduled reports by email or Slack

Also included

The unglamorous parts, done properly

SLA timers

A visible countdown per ticket and an escalation that fires before you break the promise, not after.

Collision detection

You see who else has the conversation open and what they're typing, before you send the second reply.

Saved replies

Snippets with variables that pull in the customer's name, order and plan, so they don't read like a form letter.

Customer context

Orders, subscription state and past conversations in the side panel — pulled from Shopify, Stripe or your own API.

Real mobile apps

iOS and Android with push notifications, so weekend cover doesn't mean somebody sitting at a laptop.

API & webhooks

A documented REST API, webhooks for every event, and a sandbox so you can build against it before you buy.
